# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 68 — THE ROAD REMEMBERS

### Purpose
Make The Black Highway itself a persistent survival system: routes, wrecks, travelers, hazards, memories and opportunities change over time.

### Core Rule
The road is not scenery between missions. It is a living survival space.

### Route State
Routes can become:
- open
- congested
- blocked
- abandoned
- dangerous
- controlled by a settlement
- temporarily cleared
- newly discovered

### Persistent Evidence
The player can encounter the consequences of earlier events: abandoned vehicles, repaired barriers, memorials, scavenged sites, changed signage, missing travelers, fresh tracks and altered routes.

### Travel Decisions
Players weigh speed, fuel, visibility, supplies, weather, vehicle condition, companions, information and known threats. There is rarely one universally correct route.

### Other Travelers
NPC groups can use the highway without the player. They may trade, flee, rescue others, become stranded, disappear, establish camps or alter routes.

### Discovery
A location can contain different opportunities depending on timing. Arriving early may find supplies; arriving later may find survivors; arriving later still may find only evidence of what happened.

### Black Highway Identity
The road becomes one of Chapter 3's defining symbols: movement, uncertainty, separation, reunion, escape and the possibility that someone was here before you.

### Echo Aurora
Echo Aurora manifestations may occasionally appear along the road as unexplained anomalies, but they remain separate from ordinary travel systems and are never used to explain every mystery.

### Layer Lock
> “Every road leads somewhere. After The Collapse, the harder question is who was on it before you.”

> “The highway remembers through what it leaves behind.”
