# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 98 — THE LIVING THREAD

### Purpose
Make continuation after loss feel like an organic feature of the living world rather than a mechanical character-switching system.

### Core Principle
**The story follows life, not a designated avatar.**

Every named character has their own trajectory whether or not the player is currently experiencing it.

### Living Threads
At any point, multiple lives may be unfolding:
- someone travelling toward a settlement
- someone caring for a child
- someone hiding from a threat
- someone repairing a home
- someone investigating a mystery
- someone mourning a death
- someone building a business
- someone preparing to leave
- someone making a decision the player has not yet witnessed

The player may enter one of these threads when the world makes it accessible.

### Death Creates Open Space
When a character dies, their absence creates changes rather than an empty slot that must be filled.

Other characters may:
- inherit responsibilities
- search for them
- mourn them
- take possession of their belongings
- continue unfinished work
- reject their legacy
- become closer to someone they left behind
- make decisions because they are gone

### Player Freedom
The player is not required to “complete” every character's story.

Some lives will remain partly unseen.

Some people will become deeply familiar.

Others may only be encountered briefly.

### Equal Narrative Rights
No character becomes more valuable because the player has spent more time with them.

No character becomes less valuable because the player has barely met them.

### Layer Lock
> “There was never one life carrying the story. There were lives everywhere.”

> “When one thread broke, the world did not become empty. Another thread was already moving.”
