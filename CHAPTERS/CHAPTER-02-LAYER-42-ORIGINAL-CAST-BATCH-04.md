# Chapter 2 — Layer 42: Original Cast Batch 4

## Status
**Creative draft — names and concepts added to the working cast bible.**

## Naming direction
This batch intentionally expands the multicultural ensemble. Names are selected to feel contemporary, memorable and character-specific rather than stereotypical. Cultural backgrounds are treated as part of each person's identity, not their entire personality.

---

# 31. Camille Laurent

**Age:** 33  
**Background:** French-Caribbean / British  
**Occupation:** Hotel sommelier and private-event consultant

Camille is polished, playful and extremely socially intelligent. She can make strangers feel like old friends within minutes. She has a talent for noticing tension in a room before anyone else acknowledges it.

### Secret
Camille once overheard a conversation at a private event that she was never supposed to hear. She wrote down only three words from it and has kept the note for years.

### Signature object
A tiny black notebook filled with fragments of overheard conversations.

### Potential arc
Her ability to read rooms becomes useful when several characters begin lying to one another at major social events.

---

# 32. Rohan Mehta

**Age:** 30  
**Background:** Indian Hindu / British  
**Occupation:** Emergency veterinarian

Rohan is gentle with animals and surprisingly blunt with people. He is funny, stubborn and deeply protective of his younger sister.

He has a strong sense of responsibility and hates being treated as though kindness makes him weak.

### Secret
Rohan has been quietly paying someone else's medical expenses for months without telling his family.

### Signature object
A faded photograph of his first dog tucked behind his phone case.

### Potential arc
His work unexpectedly connects him to an important piece of evidence involving an animal, a vehicle and a missing person.

---

# 33. Mina Park

**Age:** 25  
**Background:** Korean-American  
**Occupation:** Makeup artist and beauty-content creator

Mina is fashionable, witty and apparently fearless. She understands social media culture extremely well but privately dislikes being constantly perceived.

She notices faces, expressions and tiny changes in appearance for a living.

### Secret
Mina recognized someone in a photograph because they were wearing makeup she personally applied years earlier.

### Signature object
A vintage red lipstick passed down by her mother.

### Potential arc
Her professional eye allows her to identify a disguised person when almost everyone else misses it.

---

# 34. Malik Rowan

**Age:** 39  
**Background:** Black British  
**Occupation:** Firefighter

Malik is physically brave, emotionally reserved and fiercely dependable. People trust him during emergencies because he rarely panics.

He has a dry sense of humour and a soft spot for children.

### Secret
Malik has been having nightmares about an old rescue call and believes he made the wrong decision that night.

### Signature object
A child's paper bracelet from a rescue he never forgot.

### Potential arc
A future emergency forces Malik to confront the memory he has spent years avoiding.

---

# 35. Layla Rahman

**Age:** 31  
**Background:** British-Bangladeshi Muslim  
**Occupation:** Family lawyer

Layla is articulate, observant and extremely difficult to manipulate. She has a warm sense of humour that only appears around people she trusts.

She works with families in crisis and has become unusually good at spotting when someone is lying about why they want a separation, custody change or emergency legal order.

### Secret
One of her former clients left her a sealed envelope with instructions that it must not be opened until a specific person disappears.

### Signature object
A fountain pen given to her by her first mentor.

### Potential arc
The sealed envelope eventually becomes relevant to a disappearance in the wider character network.

---

# 36. Kairo James

**Age:** 28  
**Background:** Black American  
**Occupation:** Music producer

Kairo is ambitious, charismatic and intensely private. He can turn almost any sound into music and spends nights recording city noises for samples.

He appears confident but worries constantly about becoming irrelevant.

### Secret
One of his recordings contains a conversation captured accidentally in the background.

He doesn't realize its significance at first.

### Signature object
A battered portable recorder.

### Potential arc
His recording can intersect with the work of the truth-telling musician already established by the creator.

---

# 37. Yuki Tanaka

**Age:** 36  
**Background:** Japanese  
**Occupation:** Interior architect

Yuki is precise, calm and quietly funny. She sees spaces differently from most people and remembers the layout of rooms after visiting them once.

She is particularly good at noticing when something in a familiar environment has been moved.

### Secret
She realizes that a room she designed has been altered in a way that suggests someone has been hiding something inside the building.

### Signature object
A mechanical pencil she has used since university.

### Potential arc
Her spatial memory becomes unexpectedly important to reconstructing what happened inside a location.

---

# 38. Aiden Brooks

**Age:** 44  
**Background:** Black British  
**Occupation:** Luxury chauffeur

Aiden is charming, patient and almost impossible to embarrass. He has driven wealthy clients, celebrities and ordinary families for years.

He knows when to speak and when silence is safer.

### Secret
Aiden once drove a passenger who asked him to forget the entire journey.

He didn't.

### Signature object
A collection of old parking receipts with dates written on the backs.

### Potential arc
His memory of routes and times can establish a timeline that contradicts someone's public story.

---

# 39. Sana Qureshi

**Age:** 23  
**Background:** Indian Muslim / British  
**Occupation:** Graduate architecture student

Sana is bright, restless and intensely curious. She loves old buildings and abandoned spaces because she thinks they reveal the history people try to hide.

She has a habit of sketching places from memory.

### Secret
One of her sketches accidentally captures a distinctive detail from a location she was never supposed to know about.

### Signature object
A black sketchbook.

### Potential arc
Her drawings can become an unexpected visual record of changes in the world.

---

# 40. Elias Monroe

**Age:** 52  
**Background:** American  
**Occupation:** Psychiatrist and university lecturer

Elias is composed, articulate and seemingly impossible to rattle. He is fascinated by memory, grief and why people tell themselves stories that protect them from painful truths.

He is not a stereotypical sinister therapist. He genuinely wants to help people.

### Secret
A former patient left him a diary containing information that Elias believes could place someone in immediate danger.

He has kept the diary locked away because he is terrified of what revealing it could do.

### Signature object
A locked wooden case containing old journals.

### Potential arc
Elias becomes a bridge between several emotional storylines without ever becoming an omniscient narrator.
