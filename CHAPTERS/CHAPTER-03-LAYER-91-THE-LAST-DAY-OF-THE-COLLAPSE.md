# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 91 — THE LAST DAY OF THE COLLAPSE

### Purpose
Lock the boundary between the playable survival story and the post-Collapse observation epilogue.

### Core Rule
**The player remains fully interactive until The Collapse is genuinely over.**

There is no powerless spectator phase during the final crisis. The player can move, investigate, choose, speak, travel, protect, lose, survive and experience consequences through the final playable sequence.

### The Final Crisis
The last major Collapse sequence should feel like the culmination of everything the player has learned:
- roads and settlements have changed
- relationships carry accumulated history
- resources matter
- previous decisions return as consequences
- the Selection List has proven imperfect
- NPCs continue making independent decisions
- survival remains uncertain until the final outcome

### The End of The Collapse
The Collapse does not end with a simple victory screen. It ends through a believable transition in which immediate systemic danger finally recedes and people begin establishing a new normal.

### Final Player Agency
Until the final playable moment, the player can still:
- make meaningful choices
- interact with characters
- move through the environment
- protect or fail to protect people
- investigate unresolved evidence
- experience the consequences of earlier decisions
- witness who actually survives

### Boundary
Only after the final playable sequence concludes does the game stop accepting player action.

This boundary must be unmistakable in retrospect but not announced as a mechanical trick.

### Layer Lock
> “You played until the world stopped asking you to survive.”

> “The Collapse ended first. The game ended second.”
