# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 92 — THE WORLD BREATHES AGAIN

### Purpose
Create the visible post-Collapse recovery phase that proves the player reached the end of the survival story before interaction disappears.

### Core Rule
**The world must visibly return to ordinary life before the player becomes an observer.**

### Recovery
Recovery is uneven rather than magical. People repair roads, reopen shops, restore communications, return to work, rebuild homes, reopen schools, restart farms and establish new routines.

Some places recover quickly. Others remain damaged for years. Some communities disappear permanently.

### Survivors' Lives
The player can see the actual consequences of their journey:
- who survived
- who died
- who reunited
- who remained separated
- which homes survived
- which businesses reopened
- which relationships changed
- which children grew
- which possessions were inherited
- which mysteries remain unresolved

### Ordinary Life Returns
The emotional power comes from normality: breakfast, work, shopping, school, birthdays, arguments, laughter, cooking, repairs, dating, mourning and quiet evenings.

The world does not return to exactly what it was. It becomes a new normal shaped by everything that happened.

### Final Interactive Window
The player remains able to move and engage while this recovery is still part of the playable ending. Once the final playable sequence concludes, interaction ends completely.

### Layer Lock
> “After everything they lost, the survivors finally got to live again.”

> “And only when life became ordinary again did the player become a stranger to it.”
