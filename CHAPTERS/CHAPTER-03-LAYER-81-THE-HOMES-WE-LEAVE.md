# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 81 — THE HOMES WE LEAVE

### Purpose
Give abandoned homes and personal spaces persistent emotional and material meaning.

### Core Rule
A home remains part of the story after its owner leaves it.

### Home State
Track occupants, ownership, condition, supplies, modifications, evidence, memories, security and access.

### Why Homes Empty
People flee, die, relocate, join settlements, become separated from family, trade property or leave temporarily intending to return.

### What Remains
Food, clothing, photographs, furniture, documents, toys, medicine, vehicles, unfinished meals, decorations, tools and personal notes can tell the story of whoever lived there.

### Player Interaction
The player may search, preserve, take, return, repair, occupy, secure, abandon or destroy access to a home depending on circumstances.

### Emotional Discovery
A familiar location can reveal a changed life: a child's height marks, a birthday decoration, a packed suitcase, a half-written letter or a photograph missing one person.

### Ownership
Homes can be inherited, purchased, claimed, disputed, shared or repurposed. Property decisions can create long-term relationships and conflict.

### Layer Lock
> “The hardest thing about leaving home is not carrying everything with you. It is knowing what you are leaving behind.”
