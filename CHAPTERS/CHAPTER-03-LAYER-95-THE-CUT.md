# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 95 — THE CUT

### Purpose
Lock the final cinematic transition from the survivor observation sequence to the player's real-world awakening.

### Final Observation
The player watches the survivors long enough to understand that life has continued.

There is no final mission.

There is no final interaction.

There is no request for help.

There is no last-minute button prompt that can change what happens.

### The Cut
A quiet ordinary moment ends the world sequence. It should not be the biggest explosion or most dramatic death. The emotional contrast is stronger if the final image of the world is peaceful.

A survivor closes a door.

A child laughs somewhere nearby.

Traffic moves on a repaired road.

Someone prepares food.

Someone visits a grave.

Someone goes to work.

The world continues.

Then the image cuts to black.

### Sound Bridge
A familiar sound from the world becomes something else:
- distant machinery becomes hospital equipment
- rainfall becomes ventilation
- a radio tone becomes a medical monitor
- footsteps become shoes crossing a hospital floor
- a vehicle hum becomes clinical machinery

The transition should make the player question when the game world ended and reality began.

### Hospital
The player wakes in a private hospital bed.

They have been unconscious for six months.

The hospital is calm, clean and unmistakably real.

The player looks around, breathing slowly, trying to understand where they are.

Then:

> **“Where am I?”**

### Final Suspense
Before the player receives a complete explanation, the scene reveals one subtle detail that connects to something from the experience.

The detail must be ambiguous enough to support multiple interpretations.

The game ends before the player can investigate.

### Absolute Ending Rules
- No “it was all a dream” confirmation.
- No complete supernatural explanation.
- No confirmation that the player was a ghost.
- No confirmation that the player physically entered the world.
- No post-credit explanation that resolves the mystery.
- No sequel hook that explains the answer immediately.

### Final Emotional State
Relief: the player survived.

Grief: the people they watched are gone from their reach.

Confusion: six months are missing.

Doubt: the experience looked imaginary.

Fear: one detail says otherwise.

### Final Line
> **“Where am I?”**

### Layer Lock
> “The world ended. The game ended. The player woke up.”

> “And somewhere between those three facts was the truth.”
