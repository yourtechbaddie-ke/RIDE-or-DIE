# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 69 — THE PRICE OF TRUTH

### Purpose
Make information consequential. Discovering the truth can help survival while simultaneously making survival harder.

### Core Rule
Knowledge is a resource, not a free reward.

### Information Choices
The player can:
- keep information private
- tell one person
- warn a group
- trade information for resources
- use information as leverage
- publish or broadcast it
- destroy evidence
- deliberately believe another explanation

### Consequences
Truth can create panic, migration, retaliation, recruitment, distrust, violence, protection or opportunity. A false accusation can damage an innocent person's life even when the player believed it was true.

### Evidence Quality
Verified, credible, questionable, contradicted, false and unknown evidence remain distinct. The player should never receive an automatic “correct theory” marker.

### Human Response
People react according to personality and circumstance. One survivor may demand proof; another may act immediately; another may refuse to believe the player because the truth is too frightening.

### Organization Response
The organization can notice information leaks and alter behavior. It may remove records, relocate personnel, change routes, monitor witnesses or attempt to discredit the player without becoming omniscient.

### Layer Lock
> “The truth can save you. The truth can also tell someone where to find you.”

> “In RIDE OR DIE, knowing more does not always make surviving easier.”
