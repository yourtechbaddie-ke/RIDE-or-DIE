# Chapter 2 — Layer 42: Original Cast Batch 02

## Status
**Creative draft — ready for review before individual canon locks.**

## Naming direction
This batch follows the locked naming aesthetic: distinctive, memorable, contemporary English-speaking / Western-style names, with occasional surname-style given names and unusual but believable combinations. No Kenyan surnames or Swahili naming conventions are used for this original batch.

The newborn **Tariq St. Patrick** is already established separately and is not replaced by this batch.

# 11 — Sloane Mercer

**Age:** 32  
**Occupation:** Corporate event planner  
**Role:** Social connector / event-world character

Sloane can make an impossible event look effortless. She is polished, organized and socially fearless, with a habit of smiling through disasters while privately calculating seventeen ways to fix them.

She is frequently present at weddings, birthday parties, launches and private gatherings, making her one of the strongest connective characters in the social world.

### Personality
Charming, controlled, perceptive and occasionally ruthless about getting what she wants.

### Contradiction
She can orchestrate other people's happiest days while quietly feeling that her own life is falling apart.

### Secret
Sloane once altered a guest list at someone's request. She believed she was protecting a client. Years later, she discovers the person she excluded may have been trying to prevent something terrible.

### Signature possession
A pearl-handled planner containing handwritten notes that are far more personal than business schedules.

### Potential arc
Her event calendar gradually becomes an accidental map of who was where, when and with whom.

# 12 — Knox Bellamy

**Age:** 29  
**Occupation:** Tattoo artist  
**Role:** Community observer / unexpected confidant

Knox has an intimidating appearance and a dry sense of humour, but people regularly tell him things they don't tell their closest friends.

He remembers details about people because he notices what they choose to put permanently on their bodies.

### Personality
Blunt, witty, protective and surprisingly sentimental.

### Contradiction
He presents himself as someone who doesn't care what anyone thinks, yet remembers every insult anyone has ever directed at him.

### Secret
One of his regular clients disappeared shortly after getting a tattoo that contained a strange symbol.

Knox never understood the symbol—until he sees it somewhere else.

### Signature possession
A sketchbook filled with unfinished tattoo designs and notes about their stories.

### Potential arc
Knox becomes an unlikely source of information about the secret networks moving through the community.

---

# 13 — Winter Wilde

**Age:** 26  
**Occupation:** Fashion stylist  
**Role:** Image-maker / social-world character

Winter is magnetic, fashionable and difficult to read.

She can walk into a room and immediately understand who has power, who wants attention and who is pretending not to care.

### Personality
Confident, playful, ambitious and intensely observant.

### Contradiction
She helps other people construct beautiful public identities while constantly worrying that someone will discover how uncertain she feels about her own.

### Secret
Winter has been quietly collecting discarded photographs and personal notes from high-profile clients.

She doesn't sell them.

She keeps them because she believes one day she'll understand why certain people keep appearing together.

### Signature possession
A vintage Polaroid camera.

### Potential arc
Her collection can reveal relationships the player never witnessed directly.

---

# 14 — August Vale

**Age:** 41  
**Occupation:** Architect  
**Role:** Wealthy professional / family man

August is thoughtful, soft-spoken and apparently dependable.

He designs beautiful homes but has become increasingly uncomfortable with the life he built inside his own.

### Personality
Measured, intelligent, patient and private.

### Contradiction
He is exceptionally good at designing spaces for people to live together while struggling to communicate with the people closest to him.

### Secret
August has been secretly redesigning a property connected to someone from his past.

Nobody in his family knows why.

### Signature possession
A brass architectural compass inherited from his grandfather.

### Potential arc
A building he designed becomes a major location in another character's story.

---

# 15 — Faye Hollis

**Age:** 23  
**Occupation:** Pastry chef  
**Role:** Food / domestic-world character

Faye is warm, quick-witted and obsessed with making food that reminds people of somewhere they once felt safe.

Her bakery becomes a recurring ordinary-world location where characters cross paths without realizing how connected they are.

### Personality
Sunny, stubborn, affectionate and emotionally perceptive.

### Contradiction
She is excellent at making other people feel at home but secretly hates being in her own home.

### Secret
Faye receives anonymous handwritten recipe cards in the mail.

The handwriting belongs to someone she believes is dead.

### Signature possession
A battered recipe box inherited from her grandmother.

### Potential arc
The recipe cards gradually become a trail into an old family secret.

---

# 16 — Sterling Knox

**Age:** 58  
**Occupation:** Private security consultant  
**Role:** Protector / morally ambiguous observer

Sterling has spent decades being paid to notice what other people miss.

He is calm, courteous and difficult to intimidate.

### Personality
Disciplined, restrained, analytical and quietly compassionate.

### Contradiction
He has spent his life protecting wealthy clients while increasingly questioning who actually deserves protection.

### Secret
Sterling once refused an assignment because he believed the client was the real threat.

The consequences followed him for years.

### Signature possession
A small analog watch that he never removes.

### Potential arc
He may recognize danger before anyone else—but choosing whether to intervene could put someone he cares about at risk.

---

# 17 — Sunday Marlowe

**Age:** 35  
**Occupation:** Radio host / late-night presenter  
**Role:** Public voice / information network

Sunday hosts a late-night program where callers confess things they cannot say anywhere else.

She has a warm voice and an almost unnerving ability to make strangers feel heard.

### Personality
Empathetic, funny, sharp and emotionally guarded.

### Contradiction
Millions of people trust her with their secrets, but she rarely tells anyone her own.

### Secret
One anonymous caller knows something about Sunday that nobody else should know.

They keep calling.

### Signature possession
A red cassette recorder containing old broadcasts.

### Potential arc
A seemingly harmless call becomes the first clue to a much larger story.

---

# 18 — Ryder Saint

**Age:** 30  
**Occupation:** Nightclub owner / promoter  
**Role:** Social gateway / morally ambiguous connector

Ryder knows how to make a room feel alive.

He is charming, impulsive and extremely good at reading what people want.

### Personality
Charismatic, reckless, generous and opportunistic.

### Contradiction
He will protect a stranger without hesitation and betray a business partner over money.

### Secret
Someone powerful once gave Ryder a very simple instruction: **watch who comes through the door.**

Ryder has never been told why.

### Signature possession
A silver lighter engraved with initials that aren't his.

### Potential arc
His nightclub becomes a crossroads where several apparently unrelated characters repeatedly meet.

---

# 19 — Opal Sterling

**Age:** 44  
**Occupation:** Divorce mediator  
**Role:** Relationship / family-world character

Opal spends her professional life helping couples decide whether their relationships can survive.

She is calm, compassionate and brutally honest when necessary.

### Personality
Elegant, direct, patient and quietly funny.

### Contradiction
She believes people deserve second chances but has never forgiven herself for one decision from her own marriage.

### Secret
One of her clients is hiding something far more serious than marital infidelity.

Opal initially believes she is dealing with an ordinary divorce.

She is wrong.

### Signature possession
A wedding ring she no longer wears but refuses to throw away.

### Potential arc
Her professional knowledge of people's private lives makes her an unexpected witness to several relationships collapsing at once.

---

# 20 — Caspian Reed

**Age:** 21  
**Occupation:** Street musician / songwriter  
**Role:** Young artist / emotional observer

Caspian plays music in places where people rarely stop long enough to listen.

He notices loneliness immediately.

He remembers faces.

He writes songs about conversations he overhears but never identifies the people involved.

### Personality
Gentle, eccentric, curious and idealistic.

### Contradiction
He writes fearlessly about other people's pain while being terrified of confronting his own.

### Secret
One of his songs contains details from a private conversation he was never supposed to hear.

Someone recognizes the lyrics.

### Signature possession
A battered acoustic guitar covered in tiny handwritten phrases.

### Potential arc
Caspian's music can become an indirect record of events occurring throughout the world.

# Batch 02 relationship seeds

These characters are designed to intersect naturally with the wider cast:

- **Sloane** connects weddings, birthdays and major social events.
- **Knox** notices symbols and personal stories through his tattoo work.
- **Winter** accumulates photographs and social information.
- **August** connects wealthy families and physical locations.
- **Faye** creates an ordinary community location and a mysterious family thread.
- **Sterling** recognizes danger and understands security systems.
- **Sunday** receives anonymous confessions and information.
- **Ryder** controls access to a nightlife environment.
- **Opal** sees intimate relationship breakdowns others never witness.
- **Caspian** turns overheard life into music.

Together they expand the world without becoming interchangeable archetypes.

# Naming note

The naming direction for this batch intentionally favors memorable names such as **Sloane, Knox, Winter, August, Faye, Sterling, Sunday, Ryder, Opal and Caspian**. Rare-name references support the broader use of uncommon English-language and surname-style names such as Dashiell, Hollis, Bellamy, Sterling, Monroe and similar forms. The names are being curated for fictional character identity rather than as a demographic statement.
