# Chapter 2 — Layer 27 Emotional Systems Checklist

## Cinematic realism
- [ ] Every major story sequence passes the Movie Test.
- [ ] Camera framing remains naturalistic.
- [ ] Silence is preserved where specified.
- [ ] Music does not mechanically announce every emotional beat.

## Character life
- [ ] NPCs retain routines outside player presence.
- [ ] Relationships can develop without explicit player observation.
- [ ] Important characters can make autonomous decisions.
- [ ] Deaths leave persistent traces in the world.

## Emotional discovery
- [ ] Diaries can reveal information after a character's death.
- [ ] Unseen affection can be discovered retrospectively.
- [ ] Secret burials can occur under appropriate conditions.
- [ ] Protagonist reactions include restrained grief rather than mandatory exposition.

## Events
- [ ] Weddings can occur as world events.
- [ ] Birthdays can occur as world events.
- [ ] Players can attend, miss or arrive late to major events.
- [ ] Events can generate consequences without player presence.

## Violence
- [ ] No XP-based kill loop.
- [ ] Injury affects behaviour.
- [ ] Pain reactions are contextual and character-specific.
- [ ] Impulsive NPC violence can occur where personality and circumstances support it.
- [ ] Violent acts create social/emotional consequences.

## Atmosphere
- [ ] Cooking and domestic actions have believable sound and animation.
- [ ] Weather affects ambience and behaviour.
- [ ] Spaces contain layered environmental audio.
- [ ] Supernatural anomalies begin from otherwise normal environmental behaviour.

## Expanded cast
- [ ] Named-character target remains approximately 50–60.
- [ ] Characters have cross-linked relationships.
- [ ] Two celebrity concepts are represented in the narrative architecture.
- [ ] Characters remain distinguishable through behaviour, not only appearance.
