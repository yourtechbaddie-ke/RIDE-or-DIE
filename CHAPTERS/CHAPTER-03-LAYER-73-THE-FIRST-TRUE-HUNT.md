# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 73 — THE FIRST TRUE HUNT

### Purpose
Escalate the survival tension by introducing a sustained hide-and-hunt sequence where observation and preparation matter more than combat.

### Core Rule
Being hunted should feel like a problem the player must understand before attempting to solve.

### Trigger
The player discovers that someone is actively tracking them, a companion, an evidence item, or a location connected to the Selection List. The trigger can vary according to previous choices.

### Hunt Systems
- detect signs of pursuit
- break line of sight
- change routes
- reduce noise
- hide or relocate supplies
- protect companions
- choose whether to confront, evade or misdirect
- use knowledge of terrain
- recognize patterns in the pursuer's behavior

### Pursuer Behavior
The hunter is capable of mistakes. They can lose the trail, make assumptions, respond to false information, become injured, change tactics or abandon the pursuit. The player should be able to learn their behavior.

### Environment
The Black Highway provides cover and danger: abandoned vehicles, service roads, buildings, fields, drainage areas, bridges, roadside businesses and settlements.

### Violence
Combat is possible but never mandatory when a nonviolent survival solution is credible. If violence occurs, it has physical, emotional, relationship and reputation consequences.

### Investigation Connection
The hunt can reveal that the player is being pursued because of information, a person, an object, a previous action or an organization's interest. The game should not automatically reveal which explanation is correct.

### Layer Lock
> “The first time you realize you are being hunted, the road stops being a road.”

> “You do not survive the hunt by becoming the strongest person on the highway. You survive by understanding why someone is following you.”
