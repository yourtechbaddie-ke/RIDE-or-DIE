# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 79 — THE NIGHT ROAD

### Purpose
Make darkness a meaningful transformation of the highway rather than a simple visual filter.

### Core Rule
Night changes what the player can see, hear, risk and misunderstand.

### Visibility
Headlights reveal and expose. Darkness conceals both the player and potential threats. Fires, windows, road signs and distant lights become navigation clues.

### Sound
At night, distant engines, footsteps, voices, animals, doors and mechanical noises become more important. Silence can be reassuring or deeply suspicious.

### Travel Choices
The player can continue, stop, hide, seek shelter, follow lights, extinguish lights, travel with companions or wait for morning.

### Human Activity
NPCs do not automatically sleep. Some travel at night, guard settlements, search for missing people, move supplies, meet secretly or take advantage of reduced visibility.

### Emotional Space
Night creates quieter scenes: conversations inside vehicles, shared food, exhausted silence, sleeping children, arguments whispered so others do not hear, and memories triggered by familiar songs.

### Investigation
Some evidence is only noticed at night because lights, generators, signals or people behave differently.

### Layer Lock
> “The highway does not become empty when the sun goes down. It becomes harder to know who is still moving.”
