# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 74 — VEHICLES & MOBILITY

### Purpose
Make movement a core survival choice rather than a fast-travel shortcut.

### Core Rule
Every vehicle is an opportunity, a liability and a piece of someone's history.

### Vehicle State
Track fuel, condition, noise, visibility, capacity, ownership, access, cargo and location.

### Player Choices
- drive quickly or conserve fuel
- take the main road or a slower route
- carry people or supplies
- repair now or risk continuing
- abandon a vehicle or protect it
- hide it, share it, trade it or claim it

### World Persistence
NPCs can repair, steal, abandon, sell, trade and repurpose vehicles. A car seen earlier may later belong to another survivor.

### Emotional Vehicles
A vehicle can be tied to a family, job, relationship or memory. Losing it can matter even when another vehicle is technically better.

### Mobility Consequences
Vehicle decisions affect who can travel, which supplies arrive, whether a settlement stays connected and whether characters can escape danger.

### Layer Lock
> “After The Collapse, a car is never just a car. It is fuel, shelter, freedom, noise—and a reason someone may follow you.”
