# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 80 — THE PEOPLE WHO RETURN

### Purpose
Establish that separation does not automatically end a character's story.

### Core Rule
A person can disappear from the player's life and still be living elsewhere in the world.

### Returns
Characters may return because they found another route, joined another group, survived an event, followed the player, searched for family, changed their priorities or simply crossed paths again.

### Recognition
Return encounters can be immediate or uncertain. Clothing, voice, possessions, scars, habits, photographs or shared memories can identify someone before their name is confirmed.

### Changed People
Returning characters are not reset to their previous state. They may have new relationships, debts, injuries, beliefs, responsibilities, possessions or reputations.

### Emotional Consequences
A reunion can produce relief, anger, guilt, suspicion, joy or grief. Someone who was believed dead may not want to return to the life they left.

### World Persistence
A returning NPC may explain events that occurred while the player was absent, but their account can be incomplete or mistaken.

### Selection List Interaction
A person marked LOST, DECEASED or UNCONFIRMED may later reappear, reinforcing that organizational predictions are records of uncertainty—not destiny.

### Layer Lock
> “Sometimes the person you spent hours mourning is still somewhere beyond the road.”

> “And sometimes finding them again does not mean getting them back.”
