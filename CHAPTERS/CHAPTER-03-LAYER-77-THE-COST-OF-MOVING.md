# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 77 — THE COST OF MOVING

### Purpose
Make relocation consequential. Leaving is sometimes survival; staying is sometimes survival.

### Core Rule
Movement changes what the player can save, lose, discover and become.

### Reasons to Move
- supplies are depleted
- shelter is compromised
- a person needs medical care
- danger is approaching
- a relationship has broken down
- information points elsewhere
- an organization is searching the area
- another settlement offers opportunity
- the player simply believes there may be something better ahead

### Reasons to Stay
- family cannot travel
- property matters
- a settlement needs the player
- a child is settled there
- resources are stable
- leaving would expose someone
- the player has built a life there

### Relocation Consequences
Moving can cause missed encounters, abandoned possessions, changed relationships, lost property, new opportunities and different future survivors.

### Settlement Memory
Leaving does not erase the player's history. People remember why they left, what they took and whom they left behind.

### Generational Effect
A relocation can determine where children grow up, which people they meet and which communities they later influence.

### Layer Lock
> “Sometimes survival means leaving home. Sometimes survival means refusing to.”
