# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 94 — THE WATCHING

### Purpose
Define the precise post-game observation state that begins only after the playable story and Collapse have both ended.

### Core Rule
**The player does not become powerless until there is nothing left to play.**

### The Transition
The final interactive scene ends naturally. There is no sudden loss of controls during danger. There is no passive spectator section inserted before the climax.

The world has already crossed into its new normal.

Then the interface disappears.

### Observation Only
From this point onward, the player can only watch.

There are no controls for movement, dialogue, inventory, combat, driving, trading, searching, healing, building or intervention.

### The Survivors Do Not Know
Characters continue their lives without acknowledging the player. They talk to one another, work, eat, travel, raise children, form relationships, mourn, laugh and make new decisions.

The player cannot call their name.

The player cannot answer them.

The player cannot make them turn around.

### The Most Important Difference
During the game, the player could believe they were participating in these lives.

After the game, they can see that the lives continue perfectly well without them.

This creates the central existential question:

**If nobody can see you now, could anybody ever see you then?**

### No Confirmation
The observation state does not confirm that the player was invisible, imaginary, supernatural or physically present. It simply removes interaction and lets the question exist.

### Layer Lock
> “The world did not need you to keep living.”

> “For the first time, you could only watch.”
