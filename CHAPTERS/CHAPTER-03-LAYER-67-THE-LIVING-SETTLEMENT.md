# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 67 — THE LIVING SETTLEMENT

### Purpose
Define settlements as evolving communities with economies, politics, relationships and physical change.

### Core Rule
A settlement is not a menu. It is a place where people are trying to live.

### Community Systems
- Leadership can change.
- Businesses can open, close or change purpose.
- Prices shift with supply and danger.
- People work, trade and form relationships without the player.
- New arrivals alter social dynamics.
- Families can move in or leave.
- Children grow up.
- Buildings can become homes, clinics, shops, workshops, schools, churches or storage.
- Resources can be stolen, donated, rationed or redistributed.

### Social Structure
Settlements develop informal reputations and rules. One community may value generosity; another may prioritize strict rationing. Neither is automatically the “good” settlement.

### Player Influence
The player can contribute resources, work, information, protection, property and relationships. They can also destabilize a settlement through theft, violence, deception or reckless decisions.

### External Pressure
Road conditions, migration, scarcity, weather, disease, organizational activity, rumors and nearby violence can change a settlement even when the player is elsewhere.

### Physical Evolution
A familiar location should visibly change over time: boarded windows, repaired generators, gardens, new barricades, abandoned rooms, memorials, expanded shelters and repurposed shops.

### Layer Lock
> “A settlement survives because people keep deciding to stay.”

> “Every safe place is temporary. Every temporary place can become home.”
