# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 86 — THE STRANGER WHO KNOWS YOU

### Purpose
Turn reputation, information and observation into an unsettling social encounter without making every unknown person part of a conspiracy.

### Core Rule
A stranger can know something about a character without knowing everything about them.

### Possible Sources
They may have heard a rumor, met someone connected to the character, seen a photograph, received a message, traded with a family member, worked nearby, recognized a possession, or simply observed the character before.

### Player Reaction
The player can question, ignore, challenge, trust, leave, misdirect or investigate the stranger.

### Character Equality
The stranger is not automatically a villain, informant or plot device. They have their own life, relationships, resources, fears and reasons for approaching—or avoiding—the player.

### Information Risk
What the stranger knows can affect reputation, safety, trade, relationships or access. The player may learn something valuable while revealing information in return.

### Organization Connection
In some paths the stranger may possess genuine knowledge connected to the Selection List. In others, the encounter is completely ordinary. The game should not teach players that every unusual person is secretly connected to the conspiracy.

### Echo Aurora
Echo-related anomalies may create an unusual communication or visual clue, but the human explanation remains separate and unresolved.

### Layer Lock
> “The frightening question is not always who is watching you. Sometimes it is how much they already know.”
