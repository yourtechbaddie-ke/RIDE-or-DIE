# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 75 — WATER, FOOD & SANITATION

### Purpose
Turn basic needs into believable survival pressures without reducing the game to repetitive meters.

### Core Rule
Survival systems should create decisions, not chores.

### Water
Water availability varies by location, infrastructure, weather and community control. The player may find, carry, trade, share or conserve water.

### Food
Food can be fresh, preserved, cooked, traded, scavenged or grown. Cooking can become both practical survival and a meaningful social routine.

### Sanitation
Clean water, waste management, hygiene and shelter conditions influence health and community stability. Sanitation is especially important in crowded shelters.

### Scarcity
Scarcity changes over time. A location can move from abundant to depleted, forcing communities to adapt rather than simply spawning supplies indefinitely.

### Social Consequences
Sharing food or water can build trust. Hoarding can preserve a group while damaging relationships. Communities may establish rationing rules or barter systems.

### Pre-Collapse Continuity
The ordinary acts of shopping, cooking, showering and cleaning become recognizable survival versions of themselves.

### Layer Lock
> “The Collapse does not make hunger dramatic. It makes ordinary things precious.”
