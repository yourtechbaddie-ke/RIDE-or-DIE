# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 76 — TRUSTED CIRCLES

### Purpose
Define how groups form, fracture and reorganize around trust rather than fixed party slots.

### Core Rule
A companion is not a permanent inventory item. A relationship must remain alive to remain useful.

### Circle States
Characters may be strangers, acquaintances, trusted allies, close companions, family-like bonds, rivals, estranged allies or active enemies.

### Group Formation
People join groups because of shared history, protection, resources, love, fear, debt, ideology, necessity or chance. They may leave for equally human reasons.

### Companion Agency
Companions can disagree with plans, refuse dangerous actions, protect someone else, leave temporarily, hide information or challenge the player's decisions.

### Group Conflict
Arguments can emerge over food, money, risk, leadership, relationships, evidence, strangers and whether to stay or move. Conflict can resolve through dialogue, compromise, action or permanent separation.

### Trust Is Specific
A person can trust the player with their child but not with money. They can trust the player personally while distrusting the player's friends.

### Persistence
Group history follows characters into new settlements. A former companion may become a leader, trader, enemy or stranger later.

### Layer Lock
> “A group stays together because people keep choosing each other.”

> “The moment that choice stops being mutual, the group has already begun to change.”
