# Chapter 1 — Dialogue Script

## Prologue
**Child:** “Are we there yet?”
**Woman:** “Almost.”

## Mum message
**Mum:** “Call me when you get home ❤️”

## Stranger
**Stranger:** “Hey! You know what’s happening?”
**Player:** “No.” / “I don’t know.” / “What did you see?” / [silence]
**Stranger:** “The lights.”
**Player:** “What lights?”
**Stranger:** “You didn’t see them?”

## Trapped woman
**Woman:** “Please.”
**Woman:** “My daughter…”
If rescued:
**Woman:** “Blue car.”
**Player:** “What?”
**Woman:** “My daughter’s…”

## Mara introduction
**Mara:** “You lost?”
Player responses:
- “I’m looking for somewhere safe.”
- “Just passing through.”
- “Doesn’t look like anyone’s safe.”
- [silence]
**Mara:** “You’re bleeding.”

## Jace
**Jace:** “Mara.”
**Mara:** “What?”
**Jace:** “Come inside.”
**Mara:** “I’m fine.”
**Jace:** “You haven’t eaten.”
**Mara:** “Neither have you.”

## Noah
**Noah:** “Do you have a phone?”
If yes:
**Noah:** “Can you call my mum?”
Player responses:
- “I’ll try.”
- “I don’t know where she is.”
- “She’s probably coming.”
- “I’m sorry.”

## Evelyn
**Evelyn:** “First night?”
**Player:** “Yeah.”
**Evelyn:** “You’ll learn.”
**Player:** “Learn what?”
**Evelyn:** “Who people really are.”

## Dream
**Child:** “You promised.”

## Radio

**Voice:** “…don’t trust the—”
[STATIC]

[STATIC]

## Final encounter
**Player:** “Why are you awake?”
**Noah:** “Someone was talking.”
**Player:** “Who?”
