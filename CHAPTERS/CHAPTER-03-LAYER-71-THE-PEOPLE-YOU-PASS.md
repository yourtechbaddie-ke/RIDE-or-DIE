# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 71 — THE PEOPLE YOU PASS

### Purpose
Make brief encounters matter. Not every important person becomes a companion or quest-giver.

### Core Rule
A stranger can change your life without becoming part of your permanent group.

### Encounter Types
- roadside travelers
- families moving between settlements
- stranded drivers
- workers returning home
- traders
- injured survivors
- children with guardians
- people fleeing danger
- people pursuing someone
- people carrying information

### Short Encounters
Some meetings last seconds. Others last minutes or one night. A character may offer directions, trade, ask for help, warn the player, steal, lie, rescue someone or simply disappear into the road.

### Memory
The player may remember a stranger later through a name, object, rumor, photograph, radio report or another character's story. The stranger may also remember the player.

### Unpredictability
A person who appears insignificant can later survive, die, become a leader, become an antagonist, join a settlement, become a trader, or connect two unrelated story threads.

### No Quest Marker Requirement
Important encounters can be discovered naturally through observation, sound, movement, environmental clues and social behavior.

### Layer Lock
> “Some people are chapters. Some are sentences. You never know which one you are meeting.”
