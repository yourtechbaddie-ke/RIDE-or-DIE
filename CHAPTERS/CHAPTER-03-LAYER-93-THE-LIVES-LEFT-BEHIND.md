# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 93 — THE LIVES LEFT BEHIND

### Purpose
Turn the completed survival journey into a final living-world epilogue without creating another playable chapter.

### Core Rule
The player gets to see what their experience led to, but cannot continue changing it.

### The Final Gallery of Life
The camera moves naturally through places the player has known. It may revisit homes, roads, businesses, farms, settlements, schools, graves and gathering places.

The player sees survivors living with the consequences of the completed game.

### No Perfect Ending
Some survivors thrive. Some merely cope. Some relationships heal. Others remain broken. Some businesses reopen. Some homes remain empty. Some people choose to leave. Some children grow up carrying memories they did not choose.

### No Player Intervention
The player cannot:
- approach a survivor
- speak
- touch
- give an item
- warn anyone
- alter a scene
- prevent an event
- redirect a person
- open a door
- change the environment

The player is present only as an observer.

### The Camera Is Not a Character
The viewing perspective must never become a named person, ghost, spirit or hidden survivor. It is simply the player's final access to the world.

### Emotional Recognition
The player may recognize consequences from earlier choices: a repaired home, an inherited necklace, a child who survived, a grave that exists because of a decision, a business that reopened, a relationship that endured, or an empty chair where someone should have been.

### Layer Lock
> “You finally saw what became of them. You just could not become part of their lives anymore.”
