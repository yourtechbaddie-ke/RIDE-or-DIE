# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 61 — REPUTATION & RUMOR

### Purpose
Turn remembered behavior into a living social system. The world should develop an opinion of the player without reducing people to a morality meter.

### Core Rule
Reputation is local, imperfect and contradictory.

### Systems
- NPCs remember observed actions, reported actions and rumors separately.
- Reputation varies by settlement, family, occupation and individual relationship.
- Helping one group can make another distrust the player.
- Refusing violence can be respected by some and interpreted as weakness by others.
- Violence can create fear, respect, grief, revenge or avoidance.
- Generosity can attract allies and opportunists.
- Hoarding can protect resources while damaging trust.
- Rumors can be true, partly true, misunderstood or completely false.
- The player can correct a rumor, ignore it, exploit it or become trapped by it.
- Reputation changes access to trade, shelter, information, work, protection and restricted locations.

### NPC Autonomy
NPCs can spread rumors without player involvement. A witness can misremember. A frightened survivor can exaggerate. A friend can defend the player. An enemy can invent a story.

### Long-Term Consequences
Reputation can travel with caravans, radio broadcasts, family stories, journals and children who grow older. A player can arrive somewhere new carrying a reputation they never earned.

### Layer Lock
> “The world remembers what you did. It also remembers what people think you did.”

> “In a broken world, your name can reach a place before you do.”
