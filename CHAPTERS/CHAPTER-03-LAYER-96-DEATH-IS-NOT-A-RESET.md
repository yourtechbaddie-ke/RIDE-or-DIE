# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 96 — DEATH IS NOT A RESET

### Purpose
Lock the Season 1 player-experience rule that character death never requires the player to restart the entire game.

### Core Rule
**A character can die. The world does not restart.**

Death is a world event, not a punishment screen.

### When a Playable Perspective Dies
If the character currently being experienced dies, the game does not erase the player's accumulated world state.

The player continues through another available perspective or connected thread determined by the living world.

The transition should preserve continuity rather than feel like a reload into an alternate reality.

### What Carries Forward
Depending on the circumstances, the world retains:
- relationships
- deaths and grief
- possessions
- locations
- reputation
- resources
- evidence discovered
- information learned
- NPC memories
- family consequences
- settlement changes
- previous decisions
- environmental changes

### No Cheap Invulnerability
Characters are not protected simply because the player is currently experiencing them.

A character can die unexpectedly. That death is allowed to remain permanent.

### No Cheap Punishment
The player should never feel that an emotionally important death means their hours of gameplay were wasted.

The loss should change what they play next.

### Failure Philosophy
Failure creates a new state of the story rather than automatically demanding repetition.

The player may learn from failure and continue living with its consequences.

### Layer Lock
> “Someone dying is not the end of your game. It is the beginning of what their death changes.”

> “RIDE OR DIE does not rewind a life just because you lost it.”
