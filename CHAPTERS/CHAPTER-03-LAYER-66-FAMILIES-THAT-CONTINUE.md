# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 66 — FAMILIES THAT CONTINUE

### Purpose
Make family continuity a living system rather than a collection of fixed character relationships.

### Core Rule
A death ends a person's life. It does not automatically end the family's story.

### Family State
Families can experience:
- births
- pregnancies
- marriages
- separations
- reconciliations
- adoptions and guardianship
- estrangement
- bereavement
- reunions
- inheritance
- relocation
- new relationships
- changing household structures

### Children
Children have their own identities and personalities. They age naturally and remember what happened to them. A child is never merely a replacement model for a deceased parent.

### Generational Play
A future playable character may be the child or descendant of someone the player knew, but they retain their own goals, flaws, relationships and choices.

### Everyday Continuity
Family life remains visible through meals, chores, birthdays, school, work, celebrations, arguments, caregiving, photographs, stories and possessions—even when resources are scarce.

### Death and Memory
Families can preserve or reject memories of the dead. They may keep homes, sell property, give away clothing, protect photographs, destroy evidence, maintain graves or avoid discussing a person.

### Material Continuity
Inheritance can involve money, property, vehicles, businesses, tools, documents and sentimental objects. Disputes can create conflict.

### Layer Lock
> “A person can disappear from the world and still be present in every room their family enters.”

> “The story does not replace the dead. It continues around them.”
