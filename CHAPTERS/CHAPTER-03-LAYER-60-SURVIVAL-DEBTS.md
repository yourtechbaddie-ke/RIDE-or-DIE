# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 60 — SURVIVAL DEBTS

### Purpose
Make survival relational. Every rescue, refusal, gift, betrayal and sacrifice can create a debt that changes future behavior.

### Core Rule
A survival debt is not a currency number. It is a remembered obligation.

### Systems
- Rescue can create gratitude, obligation, resentment or dependency.
- Giving food, fuel, medicine, shelter, money or information can create social debt.
- A character may repay immediately, later, indirectly, or never.
- NPCs can demand repayment at dangerous moments.
- Players can accept, refuse, exploit or forgive debts.
- Debts can transfer when someone dies, but never automatically.
- A person who owes the player can still betray them.
- A person the player harmed can later save them.
- Material debt connects directly to Layer 50's economy and ownership systems.
- Emotional debt connects to relationships and memory.

### Examples
- The player gives a stranger fuel; months later that stranger opens a settlement gate.
- A rescued family refuses to help because they believe the rescue was already repaid.
- A character saves the player but asks for protection for someone else.
- A wealthy survivor buys safety for others and expects permanent loyalty.
- A child remembers who protected their family and carries that memory into adolescence.

### World Persistence
Debt affects prices, trust, access, rumors, recruitment, shelter, trade, rescues and future generations. NPCs can discuss debts without the player present.

### Layer Lock
> “You can save a life in one moment and spend the rest of it wondering what that life now owes you.”

> “Survival creates relationships. Relationships create obligations. And obligations become dangerous when the world runs out of everything else.”
