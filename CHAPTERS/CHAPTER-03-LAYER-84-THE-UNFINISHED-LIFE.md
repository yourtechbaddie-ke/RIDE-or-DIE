# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 84 — THE UNFINISHED LIFE

### Purpose
Make interrupted plans part of character continuity.

### Core Rule
The Collapse does not only kill people. It interrupts ordinary futures.

### Unfinished Things
Characters may leave behind unfinished meals, jobs, repairs, applications, businesses, celebrations, letters, school terms, relationships, travel plans and personal projects.

### Player Discovery
The player may encounter evidence of what someone intended to do next. Sometimes that intention is never completed. Sometimes another person chooses to complete it.

### Continuation
A child may finish a parent's garden. A friend may deliver an undelivered letter. A stranger may repair a vehicle someone abandoned. A partner may refuse to touch an unfinished project because it hurts too much.

### No Required Closure
Not every story receives emotional closure. Some lives simply stop mid-sentence, while others continue in unexpected directions.

### Generational Continuity
Future characters can inherit unfinished responsibilities without becoming copies or replacements of the person who died.

### Layer Lock
> “The world did not stop when they died. It simply stopped being the world they expected.”
