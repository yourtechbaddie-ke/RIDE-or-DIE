# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 72 — THE THINGS WE KEEP

### Purpose
Deepen the emotional relationship between people and the objects they choose to carry through the Collapse.

### Core Rule
Inventory is not only about usefulness. People carry what they cannot bear to leave behind.

### Carry Decisions
The player must sometimes choose between space for practical supplies and space for personal objects. This is a meaningful survival decision, not a punishment.

### Personal Objects
Examples include photographs, jewelry, letters, a child's toy, a favorite book, clothing, a recipe card, a music player, a key, a watch or an inherited keepsake.

### Emotional Weight
Objects can:
- trigger memories
- start conversations
- identify relationships
- prove identity or ownership
- reveal secrets
- comfort characters
- create conflict
- become inheritance
- connect generations

### No Forced Sentimentality
A player can discard sentimental objects. A character may refuse to. Neither choice is treated as morally superior.

### Death Continuity
After a character dies, their belongings can remain where they were left, be recovered by others, be inherited, be stolen or disappear. Their meaning can change depending on who receives them.

### Layer 63 Connection
This layer expands Possessions & Legacy into an intimate inventory system: material value and emotional value can coexist without becoming the same thing.

### Layer Lock
> “When you have room for only what keeps you alive, the hardest question becomes what you believe is worth carrying.”
