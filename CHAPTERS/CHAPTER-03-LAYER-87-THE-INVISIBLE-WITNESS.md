# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 87 — THE INVISIBLE WITNESS

### Purpose
Begin planting the endgame truth that the player's presence is real but their physical identity inside the world is uncertain.

### Core Rule
The player can affect perspective without necessarily existing as a visible person in the world.

### Early Illusion
Movement, camera framing, choices and environmental interaction should initially make the player feel physically present. Characters may appear to react to the player's actions through ordinary game logic.

### Subtle Contradictions
Over time, small details can feel wrong:
- a character never clearly addresses the player
- photographs never quite capture the player's presence
- doors or objects appear to react without a visible hand
- characters remember events differently from what the player experienced
- conversations can continue after the player leaves
- certain spaces seem to contain evidence of an observer rather than a participant

These clues must remain interpretable until late in the story.

### No Cheap Reveal
The game must not suddenly announce that the player was invisible. The question should grow naturally: **Was I ever physically there?**

### Perspective Shifts
The game may eventually allow the player to watch lives from outside ordinary character perspective. This is not a conventional ghost mechanic and does not confirm a supernatural explanation.

### Echo Aurora
Echo anomalies may overlap with moments of impossible perspective, but Echo Aurora must not automatically be declared the explanation.

### Layer Lock
> “You remember touching the world. The world does not remember your hands.”
