# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 83 — THE FIRELIGHT CIRCLE

### Purpose
Create temporary communities around shared danger, where strangers become connected without becoming a permanent party.

### Core Rule
A group can form for one night and still change several lives forever.

### The Circle
A roadside fire, generator room, sheltered business or guarded vehicle can become a temporary gathering point. Travelers exchange food, information, warnings, stories, work and protection.

### No Fixed Party
Anyone may arrive, leave, refuse participation, or remain nearby without joining. The player is one person among many, not the owner of the gathering.

### Social Exchange
Characters may cook, repair, trade, argue, flirt, tell stories, share memories, hide information, negotiate, sleep, keep watch or quietly observe one another.

### Autonomy
Events can continue without the player. A traveler may leave before dawn, a relationship may begin, a conflict may escalate, or someone may reveal information to another person.

### Memory
People can remember who shared food, who refused help, who kept watch, who lied and who left quietly.

### Layer Lock
> “For one night, strangers can become a family. By morning, they may be strangers again.”
