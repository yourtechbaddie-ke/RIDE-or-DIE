# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 88 — THE SIX-MONTH SILENCE

### Purpose
Establish the hidden real-world timeline surrounding the player's experience.

### Core Rule
The game world can feel like a long lived reality while the player's body has been elsewhere.

### The Missing Time
The player's eventual real-world reveal is tied to a **six-month coma** in a private hospital. This information is withheld until the endgame.

### No Early Explanation
The game should not explain the coma during ordinary play. Medical imagery, time discontinuities, sounds, lights, or brief sensory fragments may appear only as ambiguous clues.

### Parallel Time
The player experiences extensive events across The Collapse, journeys, settlements, relationships and generations. The real world continues independently while the player's body remains unconscious.

### Hospital Fragments
Near the end, fragments may include:
- a monitor tone
- muffled footsteps
- a nurse's voice
- a ceiling light
- a curtain moving
- a hand adjusting equipment
- a clock
- a distant announcement

These fragments should initially feel like environmental sounds from the game.

### Suspense
The six-month timeline is not presented as proof that everything was imaginary. The player wakes with memories that feel impossible and no immediate way to prove what happened.

### Layer Lock
> “Six months passed somewhere the player could not see.”

> “The question is not whether the world felt real. The question is why the player remembers it.”
