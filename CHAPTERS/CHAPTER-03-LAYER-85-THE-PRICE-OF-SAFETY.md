# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 85 — THE PRICE OF SAFETY

### Purpose
Show that protection always exists within constraints and can carry material, social or emotional costs.

### Core Rule
A safer choice is not automatically a better choice.

### Forms of Safety
Shelter, armed protection, trusted communities, organizational protection, wealth, mobility, secrecy, information and strong relationships can all reduce certain risks while creating others.

### Costs
Safety may require money, supplies, labor, obedience, silence, reputation, loyalty, restricted movement or leaving someone behind.

### Social Safety
A settlement may protect residents while controlling access. A family may hide together while becoming isolated. A powerful person may offer protection while expecting loyalty.

### Material Safety
A secure home, reliable vehicle, stocked property or valuable equipment can improve survival while attracting theft, envy or organizational attention.

### Selection List
Being selected or protected by the organization may provide real advantages but never guarantees survival. Protection can fail, be refused, be betrayed, or arrive too late.

### Player Choice
The player decides what risks are acceptable based on incomplete information. No universal safety ranking exists.

### Layer Lock
> “Safety is never free. Sometimes you pay with money. Sometimes with freedom. Sometimes with someone you love.”
