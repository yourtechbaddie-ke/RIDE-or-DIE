# CHAPTER 3 — THE BLACK HIGHWAY
## LAYER 99 — SEASON ONE PLAYER JOURNEY

### Purpose
Define the complete Season 1 experience as a satisfying finite journey rather than an endless death/restart loop.

### Season 1 Promise
The player gets to experience the full Collapse story.

They are not expected to repeatedly restart the entire game to discover a preferred outcome.

### The Journey
**Enter the world → Live in it → Explore it → Build relationships → Make choices → Survive consequences → Lose people → Continue → Discover the deeper mystery → Reach The Collapse → Play through its final moments → Finish The Collapse → Witness the changed world → Reach the ending.**

### Death During Season 1
Death changes the route through the story but does not automatically terminate the player's campaign.

The player continues wherever a living thread naturally exists.

### Replayability
Replayability comes from variation, not mandatory repetition.

Different playthroughs can produce different:
- survivors
- deaths
- relationships
- possessions
- settlements
- evidence trails
- rumors
- family outcomes
- discoveries
- playable perspectives

A player may replay because they **want to**, not because the game forces them to after failure.

### Completion
Season 1 has a real ending.

The player reaches the end of The Collapse and then receives the survivor observation epilogue and hospital reveal.

The game does not require Season 2 to make Season 1 feel complete.

### Season 2 Boundary
Season 2 is not defined in this layer.

It may introduce:
- new generations and family members
- enemies and consequences from Season 1
- different playable perspectives
- a new phase of the universe
- a central concept fundamentally different from The Collapse

Those ideas remain future development material rather than unfinished Season 1 requirements.

### Layer Lock
> “Season One is not about reaching the perfect ending. It is about reaching the end of the story you lived.”

> “You do not have to play it again to prove you survived. You already lived through it.”
