# LAYER 112 — ECHO AUDIO/VISUAL IDENTITY

## Purpose

ECHO is a recurring anomaly within RIDE OR DIE. It is not a normal character, not a region, not The Collapse itself, and not a conventional supernatural explanation.

## 1. Visual Identity

Echo Aurora's signature visual colour remains **#8FA9A3**.

Possible manifestations include:

- impossible lights
- corrupted screens
- unusual reflections
- distorted displays
- anomalous interface behavior
- brief visual inconsistencies
- patterns that seem meaningful but resist explanation

Use restraint. Echo should remain unusual because it is rare and precise, not because every scene contains an effect.

## 2. Audio Identity

ECHO may be accompanied by subtle anomalies in:

- radio transmissions
- television audio
- digital interference
- electronic hums
- spatial tones
- reversed or corrupted fragments
- environmental sounds that briefly behave incorrectly

The sound must remain distinct from ordinary technological failure.

## 3. No Cheap Jumpscares

ECHO is not a generic horror stinger. It should create curiosity, unease, recognition or impossible coincidence.

## 4. Cross-World Continuity

ECHO can appear across different locations and chapters without being tied to one geographic world.

It may recur in:

- The Last City
- The Black Highway
- Hollowgate
- The Verdant
- The Ashen Expanse
- The Drowned
- The Silent North
- The Origin

Its manifestations can change according to context.

## 5. Relationship to The Collapse

ECHO and The Collapse remain separate mysteries.

The Collapse has human, institutional, environmental and systemic causes. ECHO should not be used to retroactively explain every unexplained event.

## 6. Player Interpretation

The game may allow players to form competing interpretations. Evidence should be suggestive without forcing one definitive explanation before the intended endpoint.

## 7. Continuity

Echo appearances should be logged by chapter, location, trigger, visual manifestation, audio manifestation and player-facing consequence. Repeated symbols or behaviors must remain internally consistent.

## 8. Canonical Rule

> ECHO should make the player wonder whether reality is behaving incorrectly — without telling them why.
