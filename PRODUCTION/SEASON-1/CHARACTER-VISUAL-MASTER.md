# RIDE OR DIE — CHARACTER VISUAL MASTER

## CANONICAL STATUS
**Production reference — age-locked visual identity master.**

**Roster lock: 51 main characters + 18 supporting characters = 69 named characters.**

This file is the canonical source for character-image generation, 3D modeling, portraits, turnarounds, gameplay references, cinematics, wardrobe continuity and character customization.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
- Every human named character has an explicit age and must visibly read as that age.
- ECHO is non-human and is excluded from human age/appearance rules.
- Mara is **38 years old** and must consistently read as 38.
- The Protagonist has no fixed canon appearance: the player creates the character using the same age-aware visual system.
- No character may inherit another character's complete face, hair, body silhouette, wardrobe language or recognition anchor.
- Age is never represented by caricature. Mature means realistically mature, not artificially aged.
- Skin must show realistic texture, natural variation, pores and age-appropriate detail; never plastic-smooth or generically airbrushed.
- Clothing evolves logically from occupation, personality, weather, resources, hygiene, injury and time.
- Every character must remain recognizable across pre-Collapse, early-Collapse, later-survival, exhausted, injured, weathered and well-rested states.

## STANDARD HUMAN AGE + PRESENCE RULE
For every human character below:
- The listed age is canonical for production unless explicitly changed later.
- The face, neck, hands and body proportions must communicate that exact adult/teen/child age naturally.
- Use subtle age-appropriate expression lines and realistic skin texture.
- Do not make adults look substantially younger or older than their stated age.
- Do not confuse attractiveness with youthfulness.
- Do not confuse maturity with wrinkles.

# GLOBAL 3D CHARACTER CONSISTENCY CHECKLIST

Before generating or modeling any character:

1. Confirm canonical name and Character ID.
2. Confirm exact canonical age.
3. Confirm the character visibly reads as that age.
4. Confirm heritage/background and skin tone/undertone.
5. Confirm face structure and eye shape/color.
6. Confirm hair texture, length and normal styling.
7. Confirm body build and height range.
8. Confirm recognition anchor(s).
9. Confirm personal silhouette is unique.
10. Confirm wardrobe state: pre-Collapse, early-Collapse, later-survival, severe weather, injury/recovery, settlement or finale as applicable.
11. Preserve cumulative dirt, sweat, damage, weathering, injury and hygiene state where the story requires it.
12. Keep cultural and religious clothing practices accurate and non-stereotyped.
13. Never clone another character's face, body, hair or wardrobe language.
14. Never use beauty, wealth, race, age or narrative importance as a shortcut for hero/villain appearance.
15. For minors, preserve age-appropriate proportions at all times.
16. For ECHO, use the non-human visual rules only.
17. For the Protagonist, use the player's customization choices while applying the same age-aware production standard.

## FINAL CANONICAL RULE
**The Mara standard is now the human-character standard for RIDE OR DIE:** explicit age, age-consistent facial maturity, realistic skin and anatomy, distinct identity, detailed physical appearance, character presence and logically evolving wardrobe. Every human character must be produced to this standard across the entire game.
