# LAYER 100 — SEASON 1 PRODUCTION MASTER PLAN

## Purpose
This layer converts the completed Season 1 narrative foundation into a production-ready roadmap. Season 1 is not considered finished merely because the lore is written. The game must exist as a playable, audiovisual, persistent experience.

## Current Production Truth
The narrative architecture is established through Layer 99. The following remain to be created or implemented:
- Game-ready visual representations of the named cast.
- Character models, portraits, expressions, clothing variants and animation requirements.
- Environments, props, vehicles and interactive world assets.
- Full audio identity, environmental sound, music and vocal performance direction.
- Gameplay animation and interaction systems.
- Death/continuation implementation.
- NPC autonomy and world persistence.
- UI/HUD, cinematics, camera language and feedback.
- QA, continuity, branch testing and final production lock.

## Season 1 Production Rule
**Story complete is not game complete.** Season 1 reaches production lock only when the narrative, characters, visuals, audio, interaction, persistence, presentation and QA requirements are covered.

## Character Production Requirement
All 69 unique named characters must eventually have player-facing visual existence appropriate to their role. The player is not one of the 69 characters. There are no protected main characters or protagonist hierarchy.

A character may be temporarily controlled as a playable perspective, but that does not make them canonically more important or safer.

## Audio Production Requirement
Audio is a first-class production pillar, not an afterthought. The final game requires music, score motifs, environmental ambience, footsteps, movement sounds, vehicles, interiors, weather, radio/television, interaction sounds, emotional silence, character vocal direction and ECHO-specific audio treatment.

## Death & Continuation Requirement
Character death must not force the player to restart the entire game or wait for the story to end. When a current playable perspective dies, the player continues through another living thread while the death remains permanent and consequential.

**You lose the person. You do not lose your game.**

## Season 1 Scope Boundary
Season 2 is deliberately outside this production plan. Season 1 must stand on its own as a complete experience and end with the established post-Collapse observation and hospital sequence.

## Definition of Done
Season 1 is complete only when:
- every major production asset category has a specification;
- named characters have visual production specifications;
- audiovisual identity is defined;
- gameplay systems are implementable;
- autonomous world behavior is accounted for;
- death and continuation work without a full reset;
- persistence preserves meaningful consequences;
- the final ending is playable and correctly transitions into observation/hospital;
- QA verifies continuity across branches and character outcomes.

## Canonical Lock
> Season 1 is not a collection of documents. It is a living game that must be built from those documents.
