# LAYER 118 — CINEMATICS & CAMERA LANGUAGE

## Purpose

Cinematics must feel like an extension of gameplay rather than a separate medium. Camera language reinforces the human scale of RIDE OR DIE.

## Camera Principles

- Respect character perspective.
- Preserve physical geography.
- Avoid unnecessary spectacle.
- Use framing to communicate relationships and isolation.
- Let silence and duration carry emotion.
- Return control to the player whenever practical.

## Gameplay-to-Cinematic Transitions

Transitions should preserve:

- character identity
- clothing continuity
- injuries
- possessions
- location
- time of day
- world state
- relationship context

A character should not suddenly appear clean, dressed differently or physically restored solely because a cinematic began.

## Human Framing

Close-ups may emphasize:

- facial reactions
- hesitation
- recognition
- grief
- affection
- fear
- suspicion
- relief
- small gestures

The camera should sometimes stay wide enough to show that other people continue living around the scene.

## No Protagonist Language

Camera prominence does not establish a main character. Any named character may receive cinematic focus, and that focus can disappear when the world moves elsewhere.

## Rey & Tariq

Early scenes between Rey and Tariq should feel natural and understated. The camera may capture small similarities and maternal gestures without explicitly revealing the biological connection.

Examples include:

- Rey instinctively checking on Tariq
- Tariq relaxing around Rey
- shared expressions or mannerisms
- Rey noticing details about him unusually quickly
- his dimples becoming visually memorable

The camera must never announce the secret before the story intends to.

## Collapse Scale

Use increasingly large visual compositions as civilization changes, but return repeatedly to intimate human moments. The Collapse is enormous; individual lives remain the emotional center.

## Ending Camera Language

The final structure remains:

Participate → Survive → Finish The Collapse → World Returns to a New Normal → Game Ends → Observe Survivors → Hospital Reveal.

During the powerless observer state, the camera should communicate that the player can see but cannot intervene.

The hospital ending should be intimate rather than spectacular. After the final impossible clue appears, cut immediately and preserve ambiguity.

## Canonical Rule

> The camera can witness a life without owning it.
