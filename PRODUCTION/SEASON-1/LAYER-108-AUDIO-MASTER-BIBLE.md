# LAYER 108 — AUDIO MASTER BIBLE

## Purpose
Establish the complete sound identity of RIDE OR DIE. Audio is a core gameplay and storytelling system, not a finishing layer.

## Audio Pillars
1. Human presence.
2. Environmental realism.
3. Survival awareness.
4. Emotional storytelling.
5. Cinematic tension.
6. Intentional silence.
7. Echo Aurora ambiguity.

## Required Sound Families
- footsteps by surface and footwear;
- clothing movement;
- breathing;
- exertion;
- fear and panic reactions;
- pain and injury reactions;
- doors, locks and windows;
- cooking and household sounds;
- phones and electronics;
- vehicles and engines;
- traffic and distant movement;
- tools and repairs;
- weather;
- water;
- fire;
- wildlife;
- crowds and settlement ambience;
- radio/television/media;
- combat-related sounds where applicable;
- medical environments;
- environmental debris;
- object handling;
- UI/gameplay feedback.

## Pre-Collapse Sound
The world should sound inhabited: conversations, traffic, music from businesses/homes, appliances, schools, restaurants, workspaces, parties, public announcements and ordinary domestic noise.

## Collapse Transition
Sound progressively changes as infrastructure fails:
**busy → disrupted → sparse → dangerous → strangely quiet → survival ambience.**

Silence is not simply missing audio. It is a deliberate storytelling tool.

## Character Audio
Every recurring character needs a recognizable vocal/performance identity where voice is used. Breathing, movement, speech rhythm, hesitation and emotional response should feel individual.

## Audio as Gameplay Information
Players may use sound to detect:
- nearby people;
- vehicles;
- movement behind cover;
- weather changes;
- settlement activity;
- approaching danger;
- structural instability;
- hidden or distant events.

## Audio Accessibility
Production should plan optional subtitles/captions and meaningful audio cues so critical information is not exclusively dependent on hearing.

## Echo Aurora
Echo audio may include impossible signal behavior, unusual tones, corrupted transmissions, spatial anomalies and moments where sound seems to contradict the physical environment. It must remain unexplained.

## Canonical Lock
> In RIDE OR DIE, sometimes the sound that saves you is the sound that tells you to stay still.
