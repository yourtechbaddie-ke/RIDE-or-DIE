# LAYER 111 — ENVIRONMENTAL SOUND DESIGN

## Purpose

The world must sound alive whether or not the player is watching it. Environmental audio communicates place, time, weather, danger, occupation, wealth, decay, community and change.

## 1. Living World Audio

Locations receive evolving soundscapes rather than static ambience:

- homes
- streets
- shops
- schools
- hospitals
- farms
- settlements
- highways
- shelters
- forests
- industrial areas
- coastlines
- abandoned structures

NPC activity changes the soundscape naturally.

## 2. Pre-Collapse Sound

The ordinary world should sound populated and functional:

- traffic
- conversations
- appliances
- television and radio
- construction
- restaurants
- school activity
- music
- deliveries
- shopping
- vehicles
- public announcements

The player should be able to hear normal life before understanding how valuable normality is.

## 3. Collapse Transformation

As infrastructure fails, audio changes progressively rather than switching instantly to silence.

Traffic decreases. Shops become quieter. Communications become intermittent. Power systems fail. Emergency sirens become rarer or more distant. Generators, radios, fires, footsteps and human voices become more important.

## 4. Weather & Physical Space

Audio reflects:

- rain intensity
- wind
- storms
- heat
- cold
- water
- enclosed versus open spaces
- different building materials
- distance and obstruction

Footsteps and movement must respond to the surface and environment.

## 5. Human Presence

NPCs generate ambient life independently:

- cooking
- washing
- arguing
- laughing
- crying
- working
- repairing
- carrying supplies
- children playing
- people praying
- sleeping environments
- guards changing shifts
- vehicles arriving and leaving

The absence of these sounds can itself communicate loss.

## 6. Vehicles & Roads

Vehicles have recognizable mechanical identities. Audio can reveal whether a vehicle is approaching, idling, accelerating, damaged, overloaded, or being abandoned.

The Black Highway develops a persistent acoustic identity as routes change.

## 7. Danger Without Music

Not every danger requires a musical cue. Environmental sound can create tension through:

- distant movement
- a sudden absence of insects
- an engine stopping
- footsteps changing rhythm
- a door closing elsewhere
- radio interference
- glass movement
- breathing nearby
- unexplained mechanical noise

## 8. Echo Aurora

Echo Aurora has a restrained sonic identity distinct from ordinary environmental audio. It may appear through impossible interference, altered radio texture, corrupted electronic sounds, distant tones or subtle spatial anomalies.

Echo Aurora remains mysterious and is not treated as ordinary Collapse infrastructure.

## 9. Dynamic Silence

Silence is an active design tool. A location becoming quieter can communicate evacuation, death, isolation, danger or transformation without dialogue.

## 10. Canonical Rule

> The player should be able to close their eyes and know that RIDE OR DIE's world is changing from what they hear.
