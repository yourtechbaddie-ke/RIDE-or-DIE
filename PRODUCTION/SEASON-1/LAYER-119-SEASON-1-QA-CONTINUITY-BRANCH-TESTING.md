# LAYER 119 — SEASON 1 QA / CONTINUITY / BRANCH TESTING

## Purpose

Before Season 1 is considered complete, the entire experience must be tested as one connected world rather than as isolated chapters or systems.

## 1. Canon Continuity Audit

Verify consistency across:

- character identities
- ages and life stages
- relationships
- family structures
- occupations
- possessions
- wardrobe states
- injuries
- deaths
- settlements
- routes
- evidence
- rumors
- Selection List statuses
- ECHO manifestations
- chapter chronology
- player perspective transitions
- final ending

## 2. Character QA

Every named character must remain recognizable across their appearances and states.

Verify that no character is accidentally:

- duplicated
- renamed
- given another character's history
- assigned contradictory relationships
- visually aged without narrative reason
- permanently trapped in one outfit
- protected from consequences without canon justification

Tariq's defining facial continuity, including his dimples, must survive all approved age states.

Rey's hidden biological relationship to Tariq must remain concealed until the intended reveal, while earlier maternal-instinct clues remain internally consistent.

## 3. Branch Testing

Test meaningful variations in:

- who survives
- who dies
- who is rescued
- who refuses rescue
- relationships
- reputation
- possessions
- evidence discovery
- settlement conditions
- route conditions
- NPC decisions
- player choices

The world must respond without producing impossible contradictions.

## 4. Death Continuity

When a character dies, verify that:

- they do not respawn
- their possessions remain appropriately accounted for
- relationships react
- routines change
- family consequences propagate
- later dialogue reflects the death
- playable perspective can continue where intended

## 5. NPC Autonomy QA

Test that NPCs can continue acting without direct player supervision.

Verify that off-screen actions can produce believable changes while avoiding impossible knowledge or arbitrary outcomes.

## 6. Time & Aging QA

The primary Season 1 timeline remains approximately **12–18 months**.

Young characters must not be artificially aged into entirely different people merely because the production contains many chapters.

Life-stage changes must have explicit timeline justification.

Elderly natural deaths must be age/timeline appropriate rather than randomly inserted.

## 7. Audio-Visual QA

Verify synchronization and continuity among:

- dialogue
- facial performance
- body animation
- clothing movement
- environmental audio
- music
- ECHO effects
- cinematics
- gameplay transitions

## 8. Ending QA

The complete ending sequence must remain intact:

**Participate → Survive → Finish The Collapse → World Returns to a New Normal → Game Ends → Observe Survivors → Hospital Reveal → “Where am I?” → Impossible Clue → Cut.**

The player must not regain physical intervention during the observer phase.

The hospital clue must remain unexplained.

## 9. Player Identity QA

Verify that the player is never accidentally converted into a canonical named character.

Temporary playable perspectives remain perspectives, not protagonist promotion.

## 10. Emotional QA

Testing must assess not only technical correctness but emotional continuity:

- Does loss matter?
- Do relationships remember?
- Does survival feel earned but not morally guaranteed?
- Does ordinary life feel valuable?
- Does the world feel alive without the player?
- Do reveals recontextualize earlier moments?
- Does the ending leave the intended uncertainty?

## 11. Canonical Rule

> If a branch breaks the world's memory, the branch is broken — even if the individual scene technically works.
