# LAYER 106 — ENVIRONMENT VISUAL BIBLE

## Purpose
Define a visually distinctive world whose environments communicate geography, wealth, culture, history and the physical progression of The Collapse.

## World Regions
Production must support the established visual identities of:
1. The Last City
2. The Black Highway
3. Hollowgate
4. The Verdant
5. The Ashen Expanse
6. The Drowned
7. The Silent North
8. The Origin

Echo Aurora is not a region. It remains a separate anomalous visual/mystery language.

## Environment Principles
Locations must not feel like interchangeable generic survival maps. Every major area needs:
- distinctive architecture;
- materials and textures;
- vegetation/ecology;
- weather/light behavior;
- signage and visual history;
- socioeconomic differences;
- cultural details;
- transportation patterns;
- evidence of former daily life;
- changing post-Collapse states.

## Before → After Transformation
A location should be capable of showing its history. Examples include:
- luxury spaces becoming shelters;
- restaurants becoming ration kitchens;
- schools becoming refuge centers;
- shops becoming barter points;
- homes becoming occupied or abandoned;
- roads becoming blocked or dangerous;
- hospitals becoming overwhelmed;
- public spaces becoming gathering points or empty zones.

## Texture & Material Direction
Use rich material variation: polished stone, glass, aged wood, concrete, metal, leather, textiles, tile, brick, vegetation, water, ash, dust, rust and repaired surfaces. Materials should react to weather and time.

## Visual Storytelling
The player should be able to learn what happened by looking around before reading a quest or dialogue line.

## Distinctiveness Rule
Avoid generic "apocalypse brown." Color, architecture, fabric, signage, lighting and environmental objects should retain personality even after infrastructure fails.

## Echo Aurora
Echo manifestations may appear through impossible lights, reflections, corrupted screens, interface anomalies and radio-related visual phenomena. These remain ambiguous and must not become a conventional fantasy biome.

## Canonical Lock
> The world should look like a place people once loved, not a level designed only for the player to survive in.
