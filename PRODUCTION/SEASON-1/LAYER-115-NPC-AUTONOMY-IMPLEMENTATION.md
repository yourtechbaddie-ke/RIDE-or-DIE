# LAYER 115 — NPC AUTONOMY IMPLEMENTATION

## Purpose

RIDE OR DIE's named cast must continue living when the player is not present. NPC autonomy converts the narrative promise of a living world into a production system.

## Core Systems

NPCs maintain schedules, relationships, needs, goals, possessions, locations, jobs, family responsibilities and survival priorities.

They may:

- travel
- work
- eat
- sleep
- shop or barter
- relocate
- form or end relationships
- help others
- argue
- hide information
- investigate
- become injured
- die
- have children
- age appropriately
- change clothing
- wash and repair clothing
- acquire or lose possessions
- remember important events

## Off-Screen Simulation

The world advances while the player is elsewhere. Simulation can use efficient abstraction rather than rendering every action, but outcomes must remain coherent when the player encounters the consequences.

## Personality & Choice

NPC decisions are influenced by personality, relationships, resources, fear, knowledge, values, current conditions and previous events. No character should behave as a purely deterministic quest machine.

## No Main-Character Protection

Autonomy applies equally to the named ensemble. Being currently playable, attractive, wealthy, young, elderly, famous, a parent or emotionally important does not grant automatic survival.

## Relationships

Relationship states can change without player intervention. NPCs may become closer, distant, reconciled, estranged, protective, resentful or indifferent.

## Clothing & Appearance

Autonomous NPCs participate in the wardrobe system. They select context-appropriate outfits, rotate clothing, respond to weather and cleanliness, and repair or replace garments when resources permit.

## Children & Generations

Children have age-state transitions appropriate to the timeline. Season 1's primary timeline is approximately 12–18 months, so dramatic aging is not mandatory. Later life-stage states may be used where narrative structure requires them.

Tariq retains his recognizable identity and dimples across age-state designs.

## Rey & Tariq

Before the biological truth is revealed, autonomous social simulation can naturally create opportunities for Rey and Tariq to spend time together and develop a genuine friendship. Rey's maternal instincts may influence her behavior toward him without either character consciously understanding the reason.

## Death

NPC deaths outside player view must be recorded and propagated through relationships, possessions, locations, routines, reputation and future events.

## Canonical Rule

> The player is not the engine that makes RIDE OR DIE's people live. The world should already be moving when the player arrives.
