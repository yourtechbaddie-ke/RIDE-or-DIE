# LAYER 120 — SEASON 1 COMPLETE — MASTER PRODUCTION LOCK

## Status

**SEASON 1 PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE: LOCKED**

Layers 100–120 establish the production framework required to turn the Season 1 narrative architecture into a complete playable audiovisual experience.

## 1. Season 1 Timeline

The primary in-world timeline is approximately **12–18 months**.

Season 1 does not require young characters to become dramatically older. Aging is controlled by narrative necessity and later life-stage time jumps rather than by forcing every character through years of visible change.

## 2. Character Identity

RIDE OR DIE has no main-character hierarchy.

Every named character is a complete person with the right to live, die, choose, change, leave, return, form relationships, own possessions and be remembered.

The player has no fixed project-wide physical identity. The registry's **Protagonist** entry represents the player's customizable character slot. A player's selected Protagonist appearance becomes canonical for that playthrough only; it does not replace the fixed appearance of any established named character.

## 3. Visual Continuity

The authoritative character appearance source is `PRODUCTION/SEASON-1/CHARACTER-VISUAL-MASTER.md`.

Characters remain visually recognizable across:

- clothing changes
- injuries
- recovery
- environmental conditions
- emotional states
- age states
- cinematic presentations

Fixed-age characters must retain their canonical age presentation unless an explicit narrative time progression changes their age. The Protagonist uses the player's selected age as its active visual lock.

Wardrobes rotate naturally. Characters are not permanently locked to one outfit.

Aging does not equal visual degradation. Mature characters may remain attractive, charismatic and sophisticated while visibly developing through subtle life-stage changes.

## 4. Tariq St. Patrick Canon Lock

Tariq begins life as a newborn.

At the end of the primary Season 1 timeline, Tariq remains a very young child rather than suddenly becoming a teenager.

His defining facial features remain continuous across approved age states, including his **dimples**.

A later teenage Tariq can exist through a future life-stage/time jump while remaining unmistakably the same person.

## 5. Rey / Tariq Hidden History

Rey is not naturally barren.

Her inability to have another child is connected to a childbirth-related medical complication.

Rey gave birth to Tariq, but he was taken from her immediately after birth while she was unable to protect him. He was subsequently raised by wealthy adoptive parents.

Before the truth is revealed, Rey and Tariq can form a close friendship. Rey's natural maternal instincts may cause her to care for him with an unusual depth that neither initially understands.

The relationship must be built through believable behavior and subtle clues rather than premature exposition.

The eventual revelation must recontextualize earlier interactions.

## 6. World Autonomy

The world continues without the player.

NPCs can work, travel, trade, argue, reconcile, love, separate, form households, care for children, relocate, suffer consequences and die.

The player does not need to witness every important event for it to be real.

## 7. Death & Continuation

Death is permanent.

Death does not automatically reset the game.

When the current playable perspective dies, the player may continue through another living thread. The world remembers the loss.

Elderly characters may naturally die when their established age and circumstances make that believable.

## 8. World Persistence

Choices can permanently influence:

- relationships
- families
- reputation
- possessions
- settlements
- routes
- resources
- evidence
- organizational activity
- future opportunities
- character survival

## 9. ECHO

ECHO remains separate from The Collapse and remains intentionally unexplained.

Its visual signature is **#8FA9A3**.

Its manifestations remain restrained, recurring and internally consistent.

ECHO remains outside the human age-aware character system.

## 10. Player Experience

The final experience must support:

- movement
- exploration
- observation
- navigation
- survival
- interaction
- hiding
- pursuit
- optional combat
- social interaction
- resource management
- investigation
- branching choices
- consequence
- continuation after loss

RIDE OR DIE is a game, not a passive film.

## 11. Final Ending Lock

The complete Season 1 ending remains:

**Participate → Survive → Finish The Collapse → World Returns to a New Normal → Game Ends → Observe Survivors → Hospital Reveal → Six-Month Coma → “Where am I?” → Impossible Clue → Cut.**

During the observer phase, the player cannot intervene and survivors do not know they are being watched.

The hospital clue does not receive an immediate explanation.

There is no definitive “it was all a dream” resolution.

## 12. Definition of Done

Season 1 is production-complete when:

- all planned narrative layers are represented
- the named cast has production-ready identity specifications
- the canonical visual master contains the fixed character appearance system and Protagonist customization standard
- visual and wardrobe continuity is established
- animation and expression systems are defined
- audio and music architecture is defined
- NPC autonomy is supported
- world persistence is supported
- death and continuation are supported
- UI/HUD feedback is defined
- cinematics have a consistent visual language
- branches pass continuity testing
- the ending remains intact

## 13. Season 2 Boundary

Season 2 is intentionally outside this production lock.

Future expansions may explore later generations, older versions of young characters, new regions, new mysteries and consequences of Season 1, but those are not required for Season 1 completion.

## MASTER CANON STATEMENT

> **There is no chosen one in RIDE OR DIE. There are only people.**
>
> The player enters their lives, changes some of them, loses some of them, survives some of them, and eventually has to let the world continue without them.
>
> Season 1 ends not because every mystery is solved, but because the world has lived through The Collapse and is capable of continuing without the player.
