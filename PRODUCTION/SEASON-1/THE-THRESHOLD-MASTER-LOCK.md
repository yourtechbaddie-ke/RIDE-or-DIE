# THE THRESHOLD — MASTER LOCK

**Status:** LOCKED
**Scope:** RIDE OR DIE homepage / title experience only
**Canonical region:** None
**Purpose:** Cinematic introduction and main-menu environment

## Core rule

The Threshold is **not a ninth world** and is not geographically reachable. It is a liminal meta-space between the player's perception and the living game world.

## Locked visual Top 5

1. **The Burning Road** — first-impression image: an intact road running toward the horizon while the surrounding world appears to be burning.
2. **Impossible Roads** — reality begins violating normal road geometry.
3. **Upside-Down World** — civilization appears in impossible orientation without explanation.
4. **Floating Bedroom** — an ordinary intimate human room exists where it cannot physically exist.
5. **Wrong Reflection** — the world reflected in water contains an element absent from the physical scene.

These are the Threshold's five visual DNA pillars and must remain together as the canonical reference set.

## Opening sequence lock

The homepage opens with no controls and no conventional menu UI.

**Fire → Road → Advance → Impossible Geometry → Water → Floating Room → Wrong Reflection → Black → Title → Menu.**

Target introduction length: approximately 12–15 seconds, with a skip affordance available after the sequence begins.

## First frame

The first frame must prioritize a vast road and catastrophic fire around it. The road itself remains intact and visually readable. Fire should feel environmental and world-scale rather than like an action-game explosion sequence.

## Menu state

After the cinematic, the Threshold settles into a beautiful, quiet version of itself. The menu is embedded into the environment rather than placed inside opaque panels.

### Desktop menu placement

Left third of screen:

- CONTINUE — Return to where the world left you.
- NEW JOURNEY — Enter another life.
- THE WORLD — Observe what is changing.
- PEOPLE — Everyone has a story.
- JOURNAL — What you remember may not be what happened.
- SETTINGS — System configuration.

The right two-thirds remain visually unobstructed.

### Controls

Controls are intentionally absent during the first cinematic.

Once the menu is active, desktop/controller guidance appears subtly at the bottom:

- Arrow Up / Arrow Down — Navigate
- Enter — Select
- Escape — Back

Mobile uses touch targets without permanently occupying the cinematic field.

## Hidden continuity behavior

The menu environment may slowly change while idle. Approved anomalies include:

- the burning road briefly returning
- the impossible road appearing for a moment
- the floating room changing position
- the water disappearing
- snow falling upward
- a distant city appearing upside down
- a window turning on
- an unexplained figure appearing in a distant window

No anomaly should be announced to the player.

## Color authority

The Threshold may draw from the locked master palettes:

- Human Light
- Bruise & Halo
- Electric Mourning

ECHO's impossible visual signature remains `#8FA9A3` and must remain subtle.

## Implementation authority

The web prototype implementation lives under `WEB-PROTOTYPE/` and is an interactive reference implementation, not the final Unity rendering.

The final Unity implementation must reproduce the same composition, camera language, timing, interaction hierarchy and visual intent.
