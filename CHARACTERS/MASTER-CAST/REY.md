# Rey — Character Identity Lock

## Status
**Creator-defined character direction locked.**

## Identity
**Name:** Rey

Rey is a very light-skinned Black woman whose extraordinary beauty is immediately noticeable, but her character is defined by far more than appearance.

## Visual identity
- Very light-skinned Black complexion.
- Light blue eyes with a deep, ocean-like quality.
- Extremely long, straight black hair reaching approximately the backs of her knees.
- Tiny waist with fuller thighs and a fuller bust.
- Dimpled smile.
- Striking, sophisticated physical presence.
- Her appearance should remain elegant and believable rather than exaggerated into fantasy anatomy.

## Personality
- Compassionate.
- Seductive without being naïve.
- Perceptive.
- Emotionally intelligent.
- Warm but difficult to manipulate.
- Capable of recognizing attraction without confusing attraction for trust.

## Personal condition
Rey is barren. This is part of her personal history and can affect how she experiences relationships, family and the future, but it must never become her entire identity.

## Social effect
Rey attracts attention naturally. Male characters may secretly admire her, openly pursue her or develop complicated feelings for her.

Their attraction does not give them entitlement to her affection.

Rey retains complete agency over whom she trusts, rejects, loves or keeps at a distance.

## Character principle
Her beauty can cause people to underestimate her intelligence or assume they understand her. The game should repeatedly demonstrate that they do not.

## Long-form potential
Rey should be treated as a real recurring person within the living world. Her relationships, possessions, routines, choices, reputation and survival history can evolve over time.

## Direction lock
**Rey is memorable because she is Rey—not because the world is required to make her desirable. Her beauty affects the world around her, but her judgment, compassion and agency determine her story.**
