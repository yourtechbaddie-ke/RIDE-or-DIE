# Damian Bellamy — Character Identity Lock

## Status
**Creator-defined character direction locked.**

## Identity
**Name:** Damian Bellamy
**Occupation:** Tattoo artist

## Visual identity
- Heavily tattooed.
- Tattoos extend prominently across his body and contribute to an intimidating silhouette.
- Piercings and dark styling may reinforce the first impression that he looks almost satanic or unreal.
- His appearance should remain human and believable; the unsettling quality comes from styling, expression, presence and reputation, not supernatural anatomy.
- His hands are steady and precise from years of tattoo work.

## Personality
- Quiet.
- Blunt.
- Dryly funny.
- Observant.
- Protective.
- Surprisingly gentle with people he trusts.
- More sentimental than his appearance suggests.

## Social reputation
Damian has few close friends because people fear him.

Some believe he could use his tattoo equipment to hurt or kill someone. Rumours may circulate without evidence.

The narrative should initially allow the player to wonder whether the rumours are true, then reveal that appearance and reputation are not reliable measures of character.

## Profession as survival knowledge
Damian's professional experience gives him practical familiarity with:
- sterilization;
- delicate equipment;
- skin and wound care boundaries;
- hygiene;
- precision work;
- maintaining small tools.

He is not a doctor and should never become an implausible medical expert simply because he is a tattoo artist.

## Mystery thread
A former client disappeared after receiving a tattoo containing a strange symbol.

Damian kept a sketch of the design because something about it bothered him.

The same symbol may later appear elsewhere in the world.

## Relationship principle
People may fear Damian, trust him, misunderstand him, admire him or avoid him. His limited friendships should feel earned rather than caused by everyone being irrational.

## Name correction
The earlier working name **Knox Bellamy** is retired for this character.

**Damian Bellamy is the canonical tattoo artist name.**

## Direction lock
**Damian looks like someone the player should fear. His story is about discovering whether the person underneath that appearance deserves the fear.**
