## Continuity rules
- Do **not** add characters to make the count reach 69.
- Do **not** move supporting characters into the main-character roster unless the canon is explicitly changed later.
- Do **not** remove or replace any of the 51 main characters.
- Supporting characters remain available for relationships, events, world-building and visual references.
- **Damian Bellamy** is the canonical tattoo artist. The retired working name **Knox Bellamy** must never be used for him.
- **Sterling Knox** is the private-security professional/protector and must not be conflated with Damian Bellamy.
- **Mara** is the canonical name; do not use Raven.
- **ECHO** remains non-human and must not be rendered as an ordinary human character.
- The player has no fixed canonical physical identity. If the project retains the registry's Protagonist label, it must be treated as a player-perspective placeholder rather than a fixed visual body.

## Visual production rule
The 51 main characters and 18 supporting characters each require unique visual identities. Their appearances, ethnicities, body types, hairstyles, facial structures, clothing silhouettes and recognition anchors must remain distinct and believable. Clothing must evolve with occupation, personality and Collapse conditions rather than giving everyone the same outfit.
